About us

We build trust through quality auto work and practical skills.

CEPHACO MOTORS AND TRAINING CENTRE was established to serve vehicle owners, drivers, and young people who want to learn practical technical skills and build a better future.

We are more than a workshop. We are a place where craftsmanship, service, and opportunity meet. From repairing and restoring damaged vehicles to training learners in workshop essentials, we take pride in turning effort into results.

Our mission

To provide dependable automotive care and skills development that help people move forward with confidence.

Integrity

We work honestly, transparently, and responsibly in every service and training interaction.

Skill

We focus on real hands-on capability that prepares learners for practical work and growth.

Service

We believe great work should benefit the people and communities who trust us with their vehicles and ambitions.
