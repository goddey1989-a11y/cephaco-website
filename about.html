About us

Leading quality automotive service and practical training with integrity.

Our story

Built on skill, service, and trust.

CEPHACO Motors & Training Centre was founded to meet a practical need in the community: dependable vehicle repair services and real-world technical training that can change lives. What started as a vision for quality workmanship has grown into a trusted workshop and learning hub focused on craftsmanship, discipline, and growth.

Under the leadership of Cephas Tetteh, the business has become a place where drivers, vehicle owners, and aspiring technicians can find reliable support and meaningful opportunity. Our work is guided by a simple principle: when people are empowered with skill and integrity, they build stronger communities.

Our mission

To deliver trusted automotive solutions and practical skills that create lasting value.

We combine expert workshop service with vocational training so our clients and learners benefit from both immediate results and long-term empowerment.

Integrity

We work honestly, transparently, and responsibly in every service and training interaction.

Skill

We focus on real hands-on capability that prepares learners for practical work and growth.

Service

We believe great work should benefit the people and communities who trust us with their vehicles and ambitions.
